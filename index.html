Today

Home price = pure energy price per kWh incl. VAT, without fixed fees: (monthly spot + variable costs 0.115 + grid transfer 0.258 + energy tax 0.36) × 1.25, from your October 2026 bills. Unlike petrol (a daily price), electricity at home is billed at the average of the whole month, so the hour or day of charging does not matter and the final price is only known after the month ends. Tap a month to use its price:

The car never runs the battery fully empty: in hybrid mode it holds a reserve. Only the charge between that hold level and 100 % (about 10.4 kWh from the socket, 54 km WLTP) can be recharged, and that is what this tool counts.

Default 15 %. Check it on the display the next time the car runs in hybrid mode and correct it here if needed.

Assumptions

Santa Fe PHEV 2025: battery 13.8 kWh gross, usable about 10.4 kWh from the socket (54 km WLTP). Charging is AC only. Winter and summer consumption, and the home-price forecast, are estimates (see the notes above). Parking fees, idle fees and detours are not included.
