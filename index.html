1 · My car now

Only the charge above the hybrid hold level can be recharged (about 10.4 kWh from the socket). The hold level is set under "Car settings and assumptions" at the bottom.

2 · Where I am

Finds the nearest petrol stations and chargers for the two cards below.

3 · Petrol (daily price)

After "Find my location" the 5 nearest petrol stations appear here. Pick one and its price replaces the price above.

4 · Charging on the road (Type 2, AC)

After "Find my location" the 5 nearest chargers appear here. Pick the one you will use: its price and power are then used in the comparison.

A charger without a price is not counted, so enter the price from its app. Data: eurofuelprices.com (Open Charge Map, NOBIL, operator list prices).

5 · Charging at home (monthly price)

Unlike petrol, electricity at home is billed at the average of the whole month, so the day or hour of charging does not matter and the final price is only known after the month ends. Tap a month to use its price:

Price = pure energy price per kWh incl. VAT, without fixed fees: (monthly spot + variable costs 0.115 + grid transfer 0.258 + energy tax 0.36) × 1.25, from your October 2026 bills.

How the numbers are calculated

Car settings and assumptions

Default 15 %. Check it on the display the next time the car runs in hybrid mode and correct it here if needed. The car never runs the battery fully empty: only the charge between this level and 100 % is counted.

Charging power used is the lower of this and the chosen charger's power.

Santa Fe PHEV 2025: battery 13.8 kWh gross, usable about 10.4 kWh from the socket (54 km WLTP). Charging is AC only. Winter and summer consumption and the home-price forecast are estimates (see the notes above). Parking fees, idle fees and detours are not included.
